## What to copy / what not to copy

- **Copy:** camera height/angle (behind-right shoulder, ~35° down, ground fills the frame); ranger silhouette (orange cap, yellow tank with red/blue/cyan knobs on the side, olive uniform); HUD placement (objective top-right, timer + asset bands top-left, capsule tank + 3 round valve buttons bottom-right, 3-slot gadget belt bottom-left); speech-bubble pH badges with an icon + LTR number; translucent indicator wash over terrain with soft glowing edges; grey/desaturated unknown ground; bloom (anemones/poppies) on restored ground; comic "BOING" overshoot burst and red toast; night scene lit by indicator glows.
- **Don't copy (errors in the images):**
  - All Hebrew strings — verify/replace with the game's own copy.
  - 01: the tank shows "pH 7.0" with blue liquid; the tank's liquid colour should follow the authoritative tank pH, not a fixed blue.
  - 04: the tank gauge shows pink liquid with "pH 2" — pink is the phenolphthalein *base* colour, so pink at pH 2 is scientifically wrong. In the game the tank colour must come from the universal-indicator table for its pH.
  - Asset pH bands in the HUD (fish 6–8, bees 6–7, blueberries 3–5, school 7–8) are invented by the image model; use the bands defined in the build handoff (e.g. fish ~6.5–8.5, blueberries ~4.5–5.5).
  - The litmus strip in 01 is held/flying like a dart; in the game it is a thrown gadget whose reading is authoritative.
